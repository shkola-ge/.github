## About SHKOLA

SHKOLA brings together live teaching, individual support and an active school
community in Batumi, Georgia. Families can choose a suitable educational path
while children learn in small groups, receive support from teachers and take
part in clubs, sports and shared events.

Our digital platform is available in Georgian, Russian and English and helps
families explore programmes, meet the team, compare formats and contact the
school.

## Programmes and services

- American Preschool for children aged 4–6;
- American Program for grades 1–4;
- American Program Co-learning for grades 5–12;
- Russian Program for grades 1–4;
- Russian Program Co-learning for grades 5–11;
- individual tutoring and social-pedagogue support;
- after-school groups, clubs, courses and community activities.
